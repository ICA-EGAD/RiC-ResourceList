ระบบถาม-ตอบ GraphRAG บนกราฟความรู้ RiC-O ของหอจดหมายเหตุพุทธทาส อินทปัญโญ

    (GraphRAG question answering over a RiC-O knowledge graph at the Buddhadasa Indapanno Archives)
  • Buddhadasa Indapanno Archives (BIA), Bangkok (https://www.bia.or.th/)
  • 2026-08

  • The Buddhadasa Indapanno Archives (BIA, Bangkok, Thailand) publishes its archival description as a knowledge graph conforming to RiC-O 1.1, and answers natural-language questions over it in production. ISAD(G) metadata for 1,378 manuscript items in 11 series, 334 digitised books, and a curated layer of 344 life events, 160 persons and 247 dhamma concepts are expressed as ~100,000 triples. A question-answering service on the Archives' public website translates Thai questions to SPARQL over this graph (GraphRAG), composes the answer with an LLM, and cites every statement back to the digitised source page. Entities are published as dereferenceable linked data at https://id.bia.or.th/ (HTTP 303, content negotiation, VoID description). Two aspects may interest the RiC community: (1) a small extension ontology (biax:, 28 terms, CC BY 4.0, owl:imports RiC-O 1.1) covering concepts the standard has no vocabulary for in a Thai Buddhist context — e.g. monastic ordination and royal title conferral, where a monk's name changes with each title; (2) an empirical comparison in which a vector-RAG baseline over the same collection failed on counting, relation and negation questions that the graph answers routinely.
