ISAD(G) to Records in Contexts Conversion Skill (for Claude)

  • Matthew Bruton
  • 2026-08-30

  • A practical Claude skill that helps professional archivists map archival descriptions from ISAD(G) to Records in Contexts (RiC-CM 1.0). Based on the official ICA/EGAD crosswalk published in RiC-AG 0.1 (2025) and RiC-CM 1.0, with RiC-O 1.1 predicate names. Covers the full ISAD(G) element crosswalk, RiC entity selection (Record Set / Record / Record Part / Instantiation / Agent / Date), common problem areas (creators, dates, extent, conditions of access, finding aids), and a complete worked example. Also includes bridges for migrations that do not begin from ISAD(G): US federal records (NARA metadata) and European EDRMS standards (MoReq2010, Italian protocollo, French SEDA, German XDOMEA). Open source (MIT).
